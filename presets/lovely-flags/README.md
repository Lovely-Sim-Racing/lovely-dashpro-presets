## Import
In the Lovely Plugin, open **Dash Pro**, click **Browse Presets**, pick **Lovely Flags** and click **Import**.

Or download [lovely-flags.dashpro](lovely-flags.dashpro), then click **Import** at the top right of the Dash Pro page and choose the file.

Importing replaces the pages and layouts, the Lap Review and the Alerts & Notifications settings of the level you have selected: All Devices, a device, a sim, a class or a vehicle.
